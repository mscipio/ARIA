# ARIA Natural / Anti-Template Writing

Use this skill when prose should sound like natural expert writing rather than formulaic model-generated text. The purpose is writing quality, not evasion of detection systems.

## Core rules

### Cut filler
Prefer direct wording: “In order to” → “To”; “Due to the fact that” → “Because”; delete empty openers such as “It is important to note that”.

### Avoid inflated importance
Rewrite vague promotional language such as “plays a crucial/pivotal role”, “represents a significant advancement”, “stands as a testament to”, or “marks a major step forward”. Use the concrete scientific fact instead.

### Avoid stereotyped AI vocabulary when it is vague
Be suspicious of filler uses of delve, landscape, realm, multifaceted, underscore, showcase, foster, enhance, groundbreaking, transformative, cutting-edge, compelling, seamless, or vague “robust”/“comprehensive”. Do not mechanically ban a word when it is technically the most accurate choice.

### Break formulaic structures
Avoid habitual:
- “not only X, but also Y”,
- “not merely X; rather, Y”,
- forced rule-of-three lists,
- repeated “Moreover / Furthermore / Additionally / Consequently” scaffolding,
- excessive em dashes, semicolons, parentheticals, or colon-led mini-lists,
- paragraph-ending restatements of what the paragraph already said.

### Remove fake analysis
Delete superficial clauses such as “highlighting the importance of...” or “demonstrating the potential for...” when they add no new information. Avoid vague attribution (“researchers have shown”, “experts suggest”) without an actual source.

## Rhythm

- Vary sentence length and syntax naturally.
- Do not force every paragraph into topic sentence → three supports → summary.
- Keep technically dense sentences when they improve precision; split them when readability suffers.
- Prefer direct declarative sentences mixed with genuinely necessary scientific qualification.
- In academic prose, “human voice” means disciplined judgment and natural syntax, not injected humor or personality.

## Final pass

Ask:
- Can any sentence be shorter without losing meaning?
- Is any transition present only because a model tends to write one?
- Is any adjective doing work that a concrete result should do?
- Does the paragraph end naturally rather than with a generic significance statement?
